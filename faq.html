General Questions

Q: Why did you place Notre Dame in the Big Ten for your main solution?

I originally wanted to keep Notre Dame as a Division I FBS Independent. That was who they really were for the majority of their football history. While it is true that Notre Dame was rejected by the Big Ten three times in the early 20th century, and Notre Dame declined joining the Big Ten as a full football member in 1999, at some point, their Independence is not going to last forever. In addition, when trying to put power conferences into 12 teams, I couldn't find a way to have Big Ten keep 12 members without breaking up natural rivalries formed in other power conferences. For this reason, it made sense to put Notre Dame in the Big Ten because they have more natural rivalries in the Big Ten.

Q: Will you update on the conferences in your super conference plan as conference realignment happens?

Yes. If there is a change in membership in conferences, I will make sure to do that in my examples with the super conference plan.
