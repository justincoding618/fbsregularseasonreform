Super Conferences

This is currently where we are in terms of the conference landscape. The Power Conferences have at least 16 members. However, conference realignment never sleeps. There are always going to be teams who are looking to join different conferences. If super conferences are the way we want to go, the planned cap is to cap each super conference to 20 teams per conference. If there is appetite to do so, conference size can slightly increase the max cap to 24 teams. However, anything more than 24 would likely cause complications and problems with scheduling.

Why Odd-Number Super Conferences Do Not Work

Conferences with an odd number of teams do not work all the time. This is because if going to an odd-number of conference games, the schedules do not balance out well for all teams within a given conference. The conference opponents given out to each team are not equalized with an odd-number of teams, especially going to an odd number of conference games. In the ACC, when they moved to a 9-game conference schedule since 2026, five teams in the ACC only played an 8-game schedule compared to the 12 teams that played a 9-game conference schedule. Since 2027, all but one team played a 9-game conference schedule. The one team that didn't only played an 8-game conference schedule. By going into an even number, conferences would have the flexibility to go either an even number or odd number of conference games. With an odd-number of teams in a given conference, only an even number of conference games can work for all members involved.
