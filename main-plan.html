Main Plan

The main plan is to cap the conference size to 12 teams. While the Protect College Sports Act asks for the conference size to be capped at 20 teams, any conference size above 12 is a bit too much. A conference with 12 teams provides enough of a regular season length to play all conference opponents in a given season.

The Regular Season Structure

With conferences being capped at 12 teams, the regular season schedule would look like the following:

  • 11-Game Round Robin Conference Schedule
  • 2-Game or 3-Game Non-Conference Schedule

Determining Conference Champions

After the regular season concludes, the conference champion would be determined. If there is a tie at the top of the conference standings, tie-breakers would go as follows:

  • Heat-to-Head
  • Record Against Common Opponents
  • Conference Strength of Schedule
  • Final Regular Season Rankings
