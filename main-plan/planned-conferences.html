Planned Conferences

Since the plan revolves with having no more than 12 teams in each conference, there would be a total of 72 would be split in six power conferences. While the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12, and SEC would still be there, the Pac-12 would be revived back to a power conference. The additional power conference would act similar to how the Big East was until 2013. However, in this scenario, I want to keep the Big East as a non-football Division I conference, and make a new power conference instead. The new power conference would be called the Big Metro Conference, which would honor the old Metro Conference that ran from 1975-1995.

The non-power FBS conferences would look largely the same. However, there are also some modifications and an additional conference added for the non-power FBS conference landscape. The Western Conference would be a hybrid of the new-look Pac-12 and the old Western Athletic Conference.

There are some teams that would move up to the FBS from the FCS. Montana and Montana State would move from the Big Sky to the Western Conference. UC-Davis would make the transition from FCS to FBS since the rest of their sports are in the Mountain West. South Dakota State would move from the Missouri Valley Football Conference to fill the 12th slot in the Mountain West. Tarleton State would move up from FCS to FBS to give CUSA 12 members.
