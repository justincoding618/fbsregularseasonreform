10-Team Conferences

A good backup plan is to cap the conference size to ten teams. A conference with ten teams is enough to play a 9-game round-robin conference schedule. The Big 12 did this from 2011-2022 before expanding up to 14 in 2023. The Pac-10 also played a 9-game round-robin conference schedule from 2006-2010 before expanding to 12 teams. A conference with ten teams provides enough of a regular season length to the point where expanding the regular season is not necessary.

Since the plan revolves with having no more than ten teams in each conference, a total of 140 teams would be split into seven power conferences and seven non-power conferences. While the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12, and SEC would still be there, there would be some rebranding and forming new power conferences. The Pac-12 would rebrand as the Pacific Conference and would return to power conference status. In addition, the Big 12 would rebrand as the Big Plains Conference. The two additional power conferences would then house ten members each. One of the new power conferences would be called the Big East Football Conference. This is similar to the Missouri Valley Conference and Missouri Valley Football Conference being separate conferences with different purposes. The current Big East (since 2013) can continue to be a non-football conference that emphasizes on basketball, while the Big East Football Conference would only sponsor football. Another would be put the rest of the current power conference teams, which would go from time zone to time zone. This power conference formed would be called the Metro League. It would house the 8 current Big 12 members plus Louisville and SMU.

The non-power FBS conferences would look largely the same. However, there are also some modifications and two additional conferences added for the non-power FBS conference landscape. The Western Conference would be a hybrid of the new-look Pac-12 and the old Western Athletic Conference. The Atlantic League would have nearly all the Eastern non-power FBS teams in one conference.

Regular Season Schedule

The regular season would remain at 12 games. However, all teams would play a 3-game non-conference schedule with a round-robin 9-game conference schedule. Concluding the regular season, conference chsmpions are determined as mentioned through the main plan.
